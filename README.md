# [Project #2: Exploratory Data Analysis in SQL on World Layoffs Data]

This is a project I did that's part of the online course I took on Data Analytics from AlexTheAnalyst on Youtube

* Data was taken from the online course by AlexTheAnalyst
* Using MySQL, the project utilized joins, aggregations, CTEs, and filtering to clean and standardize the data

#### Overview
After cleaning and standardizing the World Layoffs dataset, I performed an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to identify patterns, trends, and key insights within the data.

The analysis examines layoffs across companies, industries, countries, and time periods, while also exploring the companies and years most heavily affected by layoffs.

#### Analysis Performed

This SQL query analyzed the following results:

- Identifying the maximum and minimum layoff percentages
- Investigating companies that experienced 100% workforce layoffs
- Comparing fully laid-off companies based on funds raised
- Analyzing total layoffs by industry
- Analyzing total layoffs by company
- Analyzing total layoffs by country
- Examining layoffs by date and year
- Calculating monthly layoffs
- Creating a rolling total of layoffs over time
- Identifying companies with the highest layoffs per year
- Ranking the top 5 companies by layoffs for each year

#### Key Insights

- The year with the highest total layoffs was 2022.
- The industry with the highest total layoffs was Consumer with 45182 layoffs
- The top 3 company with the largest recorded layoffs were Amazon, Google, and Meta, with 18150, 12000, and 11000 respectively
- The United States lead all countries in total layoffs, with a recorded total of 256599. This was followed by India, with 35993 layoffs.
